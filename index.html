Two silent AutoRound mistakes - and why your calibration corpus is part of the quantizer

CraftWeb

The Archsloth team had optimized with AutoRound's W4A16 scheme, then exported Q4_K_M, so the exporter quietly rebuilt a different quantization and discarded most of the search. Matching the scheme and enabling SignRoundV2 cut KL divergence from the bf16 teacher by 21-54% across ten language/code axes at the same file size; calibration language and sample interleaving materially changed results.

Why it matters: a valid artifact and falling loss curve do not prove the quantization path you intended actually shipped. Match search and export schemes, test monotonicity, measure distribution drift rather than perplexity alone, and version the calibration corpus like code.

Iris-mini and Iris-pro: open-weight search agents expose the context-management tax

Open modelsAgentsWeb

AllSpark released 35B and 397B search agents, plus an OpenAI-compatible harness, tools, context strategies, and four evals. The useful result is architectural: context management lifted Iris-mini by as much as 21.2 BrowseComp points, and a failed first attempt compressed into a note improved the retry.

Why it matters: benchmark the harness separately from the model. For long research tasks, measure steps-to-answer and context burn, preserve what was checked and ruled out, then retry from a compact state instead of replaying the whole transcript.

Agent observability that closes the loop: instrument, investigate, fix, prove

From productionWeb

Honeycomb's practical loop starts with OpenTelemetry spans for every model call, tool call, and handoff. Three fields - gen_ai.conversation.id, gen_ai.agent.name, and gen_ai.operation.name - are enough to reconstruct a multi-agent run; versions, tokens, finish reasons, tool I/O, and eval scores make failures, cost, and regressions queryable.

Why it matters: do this before production traffic. A readable trace is the difference between debugging one weird transcript and finding a repeated failure pattern, tying it to a release, and proving the fix helped without moving cost or quality the wrong way.
