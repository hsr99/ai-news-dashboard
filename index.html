K2 Horizon: six fully open Apache-2.0 models from 0.9B to 375B, training recipe included

Open modelsWeb

MBZUAI's Institute of Foundation Models released six models sharing one architecture - 0.9B up to a 375B-A23B MoE - plus the pre-training corpus, intermediate checkpoints, training code, configs, and logs. FP8 and GGUF builds with day-zero vLLM, SGLang, and Ollama support; a 'Uno' diffusion LoRA adapter claims ~3x faster decoding with no quality loss.

Why it matters: a flagship-scale stack this open means you can fine-tune, serve, and audit a frontier-class MoE with no API dependency - and the intermediate checkpoints and training logs are rare public material for studying how big runs behave.

KV cache engineering for LLM serving: twelve techniques, mapped by what they actually reduce

CraftDaily Dose of DS

A map of the whole KV-cache toolkit - GQA/MQA, multi-head latent attention, sliding windows, sparse reads, quantization, eviction, paging, prefix reuse, GPU offloading - organized by what each technique reduces and what trade-off remains. Anchor stat: one 128K-token Llama 3.1 70B sequence needs ~40 GB of BF16 KV cache.

Why it matters: if you serve LLMs, the cache is where GPU budgets live or die. This is the menu you pick from when p95 latency or cost per token blows up - and it pairs neatly with last week's batching and KV-fundamentals reads.

OpenAI's chief scientist: no lab has solved alignment well enough to keep scaling at full speed

IndustryWeb

In an essay called 'An Alien Mind,' Jakub Pachocki argues that voluntary frameworks like OpenAI's Preparedness Framework and Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy should become mandated safety bars enforced by third-party auditors - and that labs should have to publish progress on recursive self-improvement. Altman reposted it as 'an important post.'

Why it matters: when the chief scientist of the fastest-shipping lab publicly argues for external enforcement, audit and monitoring requirements are coming for everyone downstream. Teams that build eval and supervision hooks into their agent stacks now will be ahead of procurement checklists later.

Tencent open-sources WeMM-Embedding, the multimodal embedder running WeChat search at billions of calls a day

From productionWeb

WeChat's vision team released general-purpose text+image embedding models in 2B, 4B, and 9B sizes, built with two-stage training and knowledge distillation. This is not a benchmark toy: the same model family already serves WeChat's recommendation and search systems at a daily call volume in the billions.

Why it matters: if you are building multimodal RAG or retrieval, a production-hardened embedder at super-app scale is a serious candidate for your next eval bake-off - and the 2B tier is cheap enough to self-host.
